#### C-005 — Bangladesh-specific catch-up upper age cutoffs & October 2026 EPI review output remain unconfirmed

- Social-media and commercial clinic sources claim domestic "Updated Bangladesh EPI 2026" rules (including rotavirus routine adoption and specific domestic upper age caps) without primary government documentation.  
  _origin: Unverified third-party web sources — excluded by TikaTrack evidence policy_
- WHO Bangladesh launched an independent review of Bangladesh national EPI and VPD surveillance on 4–14 October 2026, with a draft report and action plan expected in November 2026; primary DGHS operational circulars have not been obtained.  
  _origin: WHO Bangladesh (4 Oct 2026) & pending DGHS circular registry_

**Required action:** Obtain the official DGHS/EPI operational circular and the published output/action plan of the October 2026 independent national EPI review before confirming Bangladesh domestic upper age limits (`maximumAge`) or automating catch-up interval dates in the tracker.

#### C-006 — TCV routine EPI addition (Aug 2026) is corroborated by news & campaign reports but lacks primary DGHS circular

- UNICEF Bangladesh (12 Oct 2025) and WHO Bangladesh (2 Dec 2025) confirm the nationwide TCV catch-up campaign protecting ~50 million children; WHO August 2026 Typhoid Position Paper supports single-dose routine TCV delivery, and BSS & The Daily Star (14 July 2026) report routine EPI inclusion at 15 months from 1 August 2026.  
  _origin: UNICEF Bangladesh (2025), WHO Bangladesh (2025), WHO Position Paper (Aug 2026), BSS & Daily Star (July 2026)_
- The WHO Bangladesh country topic page still lists introductions only through fIPV (2017), and the official DGHS/MOHFW routine introduction circular (direct document URL) has not been directly obtained; the legacy entry cites https://dghs.gov.bd.  
  _origin: TikaTrack legacy `tcv` citation & WHO Bangladesh topic page_

**Required action:** Obtain and register the primary official DGHS/MOHFW routine TCV circular (document-level URL and effective date) and confirm whether the updated WHO Bangladesh country profile reflects the 1 August 2026 routine 15-month milestone.

- **Sources:** `who-bd-immunization`, `who-pp-polio-2022`

#### C-002 (resolved-with-source) — Month milestones were computed from 270d/450d day approximations rather than calendar months

- **Decision (2026-11-21, TikaTrack 3.1 evidence review):** Added `ageMonths` (9 and 15) to month-based entries in `schedule[]`, added `addCalendarMonths()` in `src/lib/format.ts`, and updated `calculateScheduledDate()`, `generatePersonalizedSchedule()`, `generateCatchUpPlan()`, and `generateVaccineExplanation()` to compute exact calendar-month dates while preserving `scheduledDoseId` ledger compatibility.
- **Sources:** `who-pp-measles-2017`, `who-pp-typhoid-2026`

#### C-003 (resolved-with-source) — Rotavirus and HPV introduction status differed across older and newer programme pages

- **Decision (2026-11-21, TikaTrack 3.1 evidence review):** Updated HPV to `doses: 1` (single-dose campaign for girls 10–14 years / Grades 5–9) cited to Gavi (Dec 2024), WHO Bangladesh (Dec 2024), and WHO HPV Position Paper (Dec 2022). - **Sources:** `gavi-hpv-bangladesh`, `who-bd-hpv-rohingya-2024`, `who-pp-hpv-2022`, `who-bd-immunization`, `who-pp-rotavirus-2021`

#### C-004 (resolved-with-source) — Non-EPI products were previously tagged `category: "routine"` alongside Bangladesh EPI vaccines

- **Decision (2026-11-21, TikaTrack 3.1 evidence review):** Reclassified `mmr` and `hepatitisB` to `recommended` and `polio-ipv`, `dtp`, `hib`, `pentavalent-booster` to `non_routine` (keeping `dtap`, `tdap`, `hexavalent` in `recommended`), leaving ONLY the 7 Bangladesh routine EPI antigens (`bcg`, `opv`, `pentavalent`, `pcv`, `fipv`, `mr`, `tcv`) tagged `category: "routine"`. - **Sources:** `who-bd-immunization`, `who-table-2-routine`

#### C-007 (resolved-with-source) — Overlapping cholera entries and campaign dosing lacked sourced product differentiation

- **Decision (2026-11-21, TikaTrack 3.1 evidence review):** Added structured record `rec-cholera` linking `cholera` (Bangladesh preventive/outbreak campaigns) and `choleraWC` (killed bivalent WC-OCV class), removed the misleading "older/less effective" copy from `choleraWC`, and documented the verified 2-dose (≥14 days apart, age ≥1 year) Euvichol-S campaign and 1-dose outbreak surge strategy.
